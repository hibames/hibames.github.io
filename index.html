hibames/Nonlinear-System-Identification-Using-Autoencoders-for-MPC

Final-year project, team of two. Implements the Partial Predictive Encoder (Masti and Bemporad, Automatica 2021): an autoencoder that learns a latent state, a transition model and an output decoder from input/output data, then drives a closed-loop MPC.

Mean Best Fit Ratio of 0.987 and 0.980 on two nonlinear benchmark systems.

  • Python
  • Deep learning
  • MPC
  • System identification

hibames/pneumonia-detection-

Synthesis project at ENSTA-B, team of two. End-to-end pipeline on about 5,900 chest X-rays: preprocessing, augmentation, class balancing, then Random Forest, XGBoost, a custom CNN and five pretrained CNNs compared. EfficientNetB0 was fine-tuned and integrated into a desktop app.

About 91 to 92% accuracy across the compared models.

  • TensorFlow/Keras
  • OpenCV
  • Scikit-Learn
  • Transfer learning
