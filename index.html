hibames/pneumonia-detection-

Synthesis project, presented to a jury in May 2025. The goal: detect pneumonia on pediatric chest X-rays and compare classical machine learning with deep learning. The pipeline covers resizing to 224 x 224, normalization, augmentation, class balancing, stratified splits, then training and evaluation. EfficientNetB0 was fine-tuned and integrated into a desktop application.

Random Forest and XGBoost reach about 92% accuracy with ROC AUC near 0.98; the custom CNN reaches 91% (AUC 0.96).

  • TensorFlow/Keras
  • OpenCV
  • Scikit-Learn
  • XGBoost
  • Transfer learning

hibames/Nonlinear-System-Identification-Using-Autoencoders-for-MPC

Final-year project. I implemented the Partial Predictive Encoder (Masti and Bemporad, Automatica 2021): an autoencoder that learns a latent state, a transition model and an output decoder from input/output data. It was identified on two nonlinear benchmark systems, then used as the state estimator of a closed-loop MPC, with no extra observer.

Mean Best Fit Ratio of 0.987 and 0.980 over six independent runs per system.
